About This Resource

A two-pass research journal built around a shared iTaoBuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use an iTaoBuy spreadsheet. It offers a two-pass routine — discover first, confirm second — so you shortlist links before you commit to any detail.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official iTaoBuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by iTaoBuy or Google. It is not a store, a price tracker, an automated QC, a logistics estimator, or a registration offer page.

The two passes

Everything here follows two passes: Discover (find and narrow without deciding) and Confirm (reopen the current page and re-check it). A candidate travels between them on a single note, then compares against the same questions, and finally hands off with URL, specs, quantity, and unknowns.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any listing clue.
  4. Make two passes and re-check the destination yourself.

Planning example — not a live product or recommendation.
