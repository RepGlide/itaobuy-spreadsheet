Pass One — Discover

First, Find Without Deciding

The first pass only discovers and narrows. It saves candidates; it does not conclude anything about them.

Pass 1Discover

  • Focus on one category at a time.
  • Scan quickly and save only links worth revisiting.
  • Keep the shortlist small and reviewable.

Pass 2Confirm

  • Reopen the current page for each candidate.
  • Check page identity, options, price context, and images.
  • Decide only after the page is re-read.

Compare with the Same Questions

No Scores, Just the Same Questions

Compare candidates by asking each one the same questions. No rankings, no scores, no winner.

  • 1

    Where does the link open? Confirm the current destination for each candidate.

  • 2

    Which options are visible? Note color, size, version, and what is selectable.

  • 3

    What is the price context? Read currency and time, not a bare number.

  • 4

    What is still unknown? Keep the open items visible instead of filling them in.

The Handoff

Stop Conditions

When to Stop the Pass

  • 1

    Destination mismatch. The link opened somewhere unexpected. Stop and re-check.

  • 2

    Options changed. A color, size, or version is missing. Stop and re-read.

  • 3

    Price unclear. The currency or time is missing. Stop and confirm it.

  • 4

    Unknown left open. A key detail is still unresolved. Stop before continuing.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

An iTaoBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a reference to research, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official iTaoBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by iTaoBuy or Google.

Does the two-pass method verify products or provide tracking?

No. The two-pass method is a research routine. It does not act as a store, price tracker, automated QC, logistics estimator, or registration offer page. It does not provide rankings, winners, or live link status.

How do I use the spreadsheet with two passes?

First pass: browse categories and save a few links worth revisiting. Second pass: reopen the current page and confirm its identity, visible options, price context, and images before deciding.

What should I confirm on the second pass?

Confirm the page identity, the visible options, the price context, and the images — then note what is still unknown rather than filling it in.

Where do I place an order after the handoff?

After the handoff, you continue on an external page with the current URL, your selection, the quantity, and your noted unknowns. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
