Two-Pass Spreadsheet Guide

Do a low-commitment discovery pass, then confirm a few candidates on a second pass.

Why two passes

Reading and deciding in one pass makes every row feel final. Two passes keep the two jobs apart: the first pass only finds, the second pass only confirms.

Pass one — discover

Stay low-commitment. Focus on a single category, scan the rows, and save only the links worth revisiting. You are not deciding here, only collecting.

Pass two — confirm

For each saved candidate, reopen the current page and re-check it: the page identity, the visible options, the price context, and the images. Decide only after the page is re-read.

Pass 1Discover, do not conclude. Save candidates, not verdicts.
Pass 2Confirm, do not assume. Re-read the page, then decide.

A worked routine

  1. Pass one: you scan the Bags category and save three links.
  2. Pass two: you reopen each link and re-check the page.
  3. Result: only then do you decide which to continue with.
